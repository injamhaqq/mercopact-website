Which footwear buyers could your factory supply?

Compare four UK and European footwear businesses. Their sourcing policies help you decide which brand to approach and which requirements to check first.

Independent research · Sources reviewed 21 November 2026

These examples use public sources. Buying interest is unconfirmed. Each suggested next step shows what we would check before contacting the company.

What the companies publish

Zalando Private Labels

Europe · Fashion and footwear

Public evidence
The June 2026 supplier disclosure reports 68 suppliers and 126 tier-one factories, and describes consolidation of the supplier base. Zalando’s private-label supplier disclosure
What to check next
Supplier consolidation is an entry barrier. Identify the relevant brand, product fit and traceability requirements before prioritizing.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

Deichmann

Europe · Footwear retail

Public evidence
The company describes large-volume direct sourcing from producers in around 40 countries and a binding supplier code of conduct. Deichmann’s sourcing policy
What to check next
Check that your factory can supply the volume and category, then ask how to qualify under the supplier code.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

Wortmann Group

Europe · Footwear brands

Public evidence
The group describes outsourced production primarily in Asia and supplier principles based on the BSCI code. Wortmann’s supplier standards
What to check next
Identify the appropriate brand, construction, testing and supplier-audit requirements.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

Clarks

UK · Footwear

Public evidence
The sourcing page describes long-standing supplier relationships and includes historical spring/summer 2022 figures. Clarks’ sourcing disclosure
What to check next
Treat historical sourcing data as background only. Ask for the current supplier requirements before preparing an introduction.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

What we would prioritize

Start with the brand whose footwear your factory can make at the required volume. Check traceability, audits and who handles supplier approval before sending a sample.
