UK and European footwear buyer research

A buyer’s footwear assortment is a starting signal. Supplier eligibility depends on construction, materials, traceability and the company’s current buying process.

Independent research example

This example uses public information to show how we evaluate buyer fit. Current purchasing plans and supplier requirements should be confirmed before outreach.

What public disclosures can tell you

Company Public evidence What to verify Source
Zalando Publishes supplier information for its private-label business. Product category, current supplier approval and material traceability. Private-label suppliers
Deichmann Describes its international sourcing and supplier relationships. Volume, onboarding, relevant audits and technical requirements. Global business
Wortmann Group Describes sourcing and supplier responsibility for its footwear brands. Which brand fits, its product range and the applicable testing requirements. Social and environmental responsibility
Clarks Publishes manufacturing information and an animal welfare policy. Current material, sourcing and certification requirements for the specific product. Where we make our shoes · Animal welfare policy

Questions to resolve before outreach

Verify footwear construction, sizes, minimums and testing. Confirm which material declarations, tannery records or audits the buyer needs. Ask which team owns supplier evaluation.
