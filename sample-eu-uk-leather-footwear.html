UK and European footwear: assess supplier access

Examine published sourcing policies and the barriers to supplying established footwear businesses.

Independent research · Sources reviewed 20 November 2026

These examples demonstrate our method, not client results. No company is presented as a confirmed buyer for a new supplier, and no outreach is claimed.

Evidence and commercial assessment

Zalando Private Labels

Europe · Fashion and footwear

Public evidence
The June 2026 supplier disclosure reports 68 suppliers and 126 tier-one factories, and describes consolidation of the supplier base. Open original source
Commercial judgment and next action
Supplier consolidation is an entry barrier. Identify the relevant brand, product fit and traceability requirements before prioritizing.
Status
Research only · purchasing intent unconfirmed

Deichmann

Europe · Footwear retail

Public evidence
The company describes large-volume direct sourcing from producers in around 40 countries and a binding supplier code of conduct. Open original source
Commercial judgment and next action
Check production capacity, category, audit readiness and buying route.
Status
Research only · purchasing intent unconfirmed

Wortmann Group

Europe · Footwear brands

Public evidence
The group describes outsourced production primarily in Asia and supplier principles based on the BSCI code. Open original source
Commercial judgment and next action
Identify the appropriate brand, construction, testing and supplier-audit requirements.
Status
Research only · purchasing intent unconfirmed

Clarks

UK · Footwear

Public evidence
The sourcing page describes long-standing supplier relationships and includes historical spring/summer 2022 figures. Open original source
Commercial judgment and next action
Treat historical sourcing data as background only. Refresh requirements before treating this account as a priority.
Status
Research only · purchasing intent unconfirmed

What we would prioritize

A visible footwear assortment is insufficient. Product-specific capability, compliance and a credible supplier access route determine whether an account is worth further research.
