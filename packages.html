Frequently asked questions

Before you engage.

Which engagement should I choose?

Choose the Sprint for a specific urgent opportunity or decision. Choose the 30-day programme if you need a buyer pipeline and approved outreach. Choose the Desk for continuous commercial work. For complex projects, discuss a tailored mandate.

Will I own the research?

You receive the contracted written research, pipeline and contact handoff as agreed, after final payment. Examples of our research and reporting are available before you commit.

Are these guaranteed buyers or orders?

No. Research fit is not the same as buying intent. We distinguish sourced evidence from actual responses, RFQs and commercial commitments. The quality and completion of agreed deliverables are our responsibility.

What do I pay for separately?

Prices are in USD. Applicable taxes and approved third-party expenses such as samples, testing, travel, client-owned sending infrastructure or specialist advice are confirmed separately before any cost is incurred.

Can you help with both public tenders and private RFQs?

Yes, we can research and qualify relevant opportunities and requirements. Bid submission, regulated procurement advice, factory audits and legal representation are not included unless a separate qualified scope is agreed.
