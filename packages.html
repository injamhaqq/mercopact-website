Yes. Send your product and target country, and we can confirm scope by message.

When does delivery start?

Do you guarantee buyer replies?

We commit to the agreed work and correction terms. Buyers decide whether to respond, request samples or place orders.

The brief is a one-time engagement. Any further work requires a separate agreement.
