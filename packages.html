Before you commit

Is the Pilot right for my company?

It’s designed for export-ready manufacturers and specialist B2B suppliers with one product and a target country in mind. You’ll need someone who can handle buyer enquiries, approve outreach and respond with quotes or samples. Use the Brief if your team wants to handle outreach itself. If you’re still choosing a country, ask about a specialist market assessment.

What do I need to share, and do we need a call?

Start with your product and target country. A website link is optional. We can confirm fit and scope by message. Before research starts, we agree product specifications, price range, minimum order, capacity, lead times and relevant certifications.

What can I review before paying?

See the public buyer research example and the written scope for your product before you approve the project. The sample shows how we explain fit, cite sources and identify what your team should verify with the buyer.

When does outreach start, and who approves it?

Outreach starts after the second payment clears and the sender, channels, messages and necessary access are approved. Your team approves individual approaches and remains responsible for product claims, prices, quotations and samples. Delayed approvals or access can move the schedule.

What if an account is a duplicate or doesn’t fit?

For the Brief and Pilot, flag accounts that fail the written fit criteria within five business days of the research handoff. We replace affected accounts within five business days of confirming the discrepancy. If we cannot meet the agreed count, we discuss a written scope adjustment or a proportional refund for missing accepted accounts. Read the terms.

Are these companies actively buying? Do you guarantee meetings?

We rank accounts by evidence that your product could fit. We record purchasing interest when a buyer directly expresses it, such as by requesting a sample or quote. Replies, meetings and orders depend on buyer decisions.

Can I use the Brief myself or add outreach later?

Yes. The $600 Brief is a one-time engagement and your team can work the shortlist itself. If you already have a completed Brief, ask about the separately scoped $1,800 Buyer Outreach Sprint.

What happens at the end of the Pilot?

Your team owns the register and keeps the source links, approved messages, response history, open follow-ups and eight reserves. We review owners and next actions together at handoff. Any monthly work requires a separate agreement.
