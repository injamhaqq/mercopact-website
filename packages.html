Frequently asked questions

What happens after the first month?

We review the work and open opportunities. Ongoing support is a separate agreement; there is no automatic renewal.

Who owns the research?

You receive contracted records under agreed payment terms. Your confidential information and commercial relationships remain yours. MERCOPACT retains independently developed methods and infrastructure.

Can you guarantee orders or meetings?

No. We commit to scope and evidence quality, not a buyer's independent decision.

When does delivery start?

After written scope, verified delivery capacity, cleared payment and usable inputs.

How do we pay?

Once scope is agreed, we issue an invoice with verified business payment instructions. Do not send funds until you receive and verify the invoice.
