Before you request a Brief

Is this right for my company?

The Brief is designed for export-ready manufacturers and specialist B2B suppliers with a defined product and someone who can handle buyer enquiries, quotes and samples. Start with one target country. We review your product and market before invoicing.

What do I need to share to get started?

For the first enquiry, share your product and target country. A website link is optional. Before research begins, we agree on product specifications, price range, minimum order quantity, production capacity, lead times and any relevant certifications.

What will I get that a directory can’t give me?

A shortlist ranked against your product and capacity, with the original sources, why each account may fit, the buying roles to look for and what to confirm before outreach. You also receive five prioritized next actions and a 20-minute handoff. See the sample brief.

Will I get direct emails or introductions?

The Brief identifies the relevant buying roles and questions to verify at each company. A list of personal emails, introductions and outreach are outside the $600 scope. If you want help approaching accounts, see the separate Buyer Outreach Sprint.

Can I agree the scope without a call?

Yes. Send your product and target country. We can review fit and confirm the scope by message. You can check the criteria and deliverables before deciding whether to pay the invoice.

When do I pay and when will I receive the Brief?

Pay $600 USD upfront once the scope is agreed. Delivery takes seven business days after payment clears and we receive the agreed information. The first enquiry starts the fit review.

What if an account is a duplicate or doesn’t fit?

Send one consolidated list of factual issues within five business days of handoff. We replace accounts that fail the written criteria within five business days of confirming the discrepancy. If we cannot meet the agreed count, we discuss a written scope adjustment or a proportional refund for missing accepted accounts. Read the terms.

Are these buyers actively looking for suppliers?

The Brief ranks companies by evidence of fit. Purchasing interest is confirmed only by direct evidence from the buyer, such as a request for samples or a quotation. Buyer replies, meetings and orders depend on the buyer’s decision.

Can my team use the Brief without buying the Sprint?

Yes. The Brief is a one-time engagement. Your team can work the shortlist itself. Outreach and ongoing support require separate agreements.

Who approves outreach, quotes and samples?

If you commission the Sprint, you approve each approach before it goes live. Your team handles product claims, pricing, quotations, samples and commercial commitments.
