Before you commit

What do you need from me?

For a new market, start with your product and target country. Before research, we agree your specifications, price range, minimum order, capacity, lead times and relevant certifications. For open quotes, start with your product, roughly how many enquiries you have and where one conversation stopped. We agree how to share private records before exchanging them.

What can I review before paying?

You can see the sample buyer record and a written scope covering the work, fee and dates. For the Buyer Development Pilot, you also receive the shortlist and proposed messages before the second payment is due.

Who approves the messages, specifications and prices?

You approve each buyer-facing message and retain control of prices, samples and commercial commitments. With your permission, we can use AI to organize correspondence and prepare drafts. We check the details against your records and carry out the follow-up you approve.

What if a buyer stays quiet?

You see the last contact, any reply and the recommended next action, including when to revisit or close the enquiry. We record buying interest when a buyer expresses it. Buyers decide whether to reply, meet or order.

What if a researched company doesn’t fit?

For the Pilot and Brief, flag duplicates or companies outside your agreed criteria within five business days of research handoff. We replace affected entries within five business days of confirming the discrepancy. If we cannot fill the shortlist, we discuss an adjusted scope or a proportional refund for the missing entries.
