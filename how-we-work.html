Request a Brief

From product specification to buyer shortlist

1. Define the assignment

You share your product, target country, commercial constraints and buyer criteria. We confirm what can be verified and send a scope.

2. Research and rank accounts

We check original sources for buyer type, product fit and relevant buying roles. We record what’s known, what’s uncertain and what to check next.

3. Review the brief together

You receive 12 researched accounts and five prioritized next actions. We discuss the shortlist with you and hand it over.

4. Approve outreach if the shortlist supports it

In a separate sprint, we prepare individual approaches for your approval and log actual replies. Your team handles quotations, samples and commercial commitments.

5. Decide whether to continue

We review the evidence, replies and pipeline with you. Ongoing development is agreed separately after a completed sprint.
