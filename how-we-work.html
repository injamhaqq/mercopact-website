How we work

A disciplined approach to market development.

MERCOPACT combines focused market intelligence, target-account development, decision-maker research, commercial outreach, qualification and structured follow-up.

Operating rule

One product. One buyer profile. One market. Expand only after the commercial thesis is clear.

01

Define

Clarify what you sell, the commercial objective, target geography, ideal buyer profile, current proof and practical constraints.

02

Map

Research the market, identify target accounts, prioritize companies and map decision-makers connected to the buying process.

03

Reach

Conduct appropriate commercial outreach with messaging based on the real offer, buyer context and relevance, not generic volume.

04

Qualify

Assess need, fit, timing, commercial potential and next-step quality before treating a conversation as a pipeline opportunity.

05

Develop

Coordinate meetings and structured follow-up to help the account move from first conversation toward a real commercial decision.

MERCOPACT does not sell databases. We develop commercial opportunities.
02

After a buyer replies

The work continues until the opportunity has a clear next state.

Positive ReplyQualificationMeetingRFQSampleQuotationFollow-UpOpportunity

MERCOPACT records the next action for qualified opportunities so replies do not disappear into inboxes. The client remains responsible for technical answers, quotations, commercial decisions, samples, negotiation, production and shipment.

Transparency

Know what is being developed and why.

Market-development work should be inspectable. The client should understand the target-account logic, outreach status, qualification context, next steps and where commercial progress is, or is not, happening.
