Request a Brief

From your product to a buyer pipeline you keep

Here’s what you provide, what you approve, and what your team keeps at the end of the engagement.

1. Define the assignment

Share your product and target country. We confirm the accounts we’ll look for and the information we need from you. You see the written scope before we invoice you.

2. Research and rank accounts

Once payment clears and we have your agreed inputs, we start the seven-business-day delivery window. We check original sources for buyer type, product fit and relevant buying roles. Each account shows what the sources support and what you still need to ask.

3. Review the shortlist together

In the Brief, you receive 12 ranked accounts and five next actions. In the Pilot, you review 20 ranked accounts, choose the priorities for outreach with us and keep eight accounts in reserve.

4. Approve outreach if the shortlist supports it

In the Pilot or Sprint, we prepare individual approaches for your approval. The buyer register tracks replies, quotation or sample requests, and who owns each next action. Your team handles commercial commitments.

5. Take over the buyer register

In the Pilot handoff, we review the approved messages, eight reserve accounts and any follow-ups still open. You keep the register in the format agreed at kickoff. We walk your team through the next steps.
