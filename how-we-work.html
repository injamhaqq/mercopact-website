How we find and approach buyers for your product

You decide what makes a buyer a fit. We check the evidence and show you who to approach first. If you already have open quotes, see how we follow up on them.

1. Agree which buyers to look for

Share your product and target country. We agree who buys this product, the order sizes you can supply and what would rule a company out. You see the written scope before we invoice you.

2. Check and rank the companies

Your shortlist is due within seven business days of cleared kickoff payment and complete product details. Each company comes with a source, a reason it fits and a question to check with its buying team.

3. Review the shortlist together

In the Brief, you receive 12 ranked accounts and five next actions. In the Pilot, you review 20 ranked accounts, choose the priorities for outreach with us and keep eight accounts in reserve.

4. Approve who we contact and what we say

In the Pilot or Buyer Outreach Sprint, you approve the recipient and the message. We send the approved approaches and record replies, quote requests and sample requests. You set prices and decide what to offer.

5. Take over the buyer register

In the Pilot handoff, we review the approved messages, eight reserve accounts and any follow-ups still open. You keep the register in the format agreed at kickoff. We walk your team through the next steps.
