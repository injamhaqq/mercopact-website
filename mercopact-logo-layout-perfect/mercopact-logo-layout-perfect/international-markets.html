Develop buyers beyond your home market.

Core principle

Market expansion begins with a specific product, buyer profile and geography—not a generic list of overseas contacts.

International buyer development

Market selection

Selected markets, not everywhere at once.

International development works best when product, market and buyer profile are deliberately narrowed. Mercopact can support selected-market research and account development where there is a credible commercial thesis.

  • Buyer profile and route-to-market definition
  • Account universe and prioritization
  • Decision-maker mapping
  • Commercial outreach
  • Qualification and meeting development
  • Structured follow-up and reporting
Selected markets · disciplined execution

Commercial reality

Market access creates opportunity. It does not remove the need to compete.

A qualified overseas account still evaluates product fit, price, quality, compliance, terms, responsiveness and delivery capability. Mercopact develops the commercial opportunity; the client remains responsible for the offer and execution.
