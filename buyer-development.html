Buyer development

Develop the right B2B accounts.

Mercopact helps established manufacturers and B2B suppliers create a more systematic route to new corporate buyers through account research, decision-maker mapping, commercial outreach and structured follow-up.

Designed for

Credible B2B companies with a proven offer, existing customers and capacity for additional business.

The objective

Move from opportunistic prospecting to focused account development.

Buyer development is not a database exercise. It is a sequence: define the right account profile, map the market, reach relevant decision-makers, qualify commercial relevance and keep the conversation moving.

Mercopact is best suited to businesses where one additional B2B customer can create meaningful recurring value.
