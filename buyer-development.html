Is the Brief right for your team?

It suits manufacturers and specialist B2B suppliers with a product ready to export and someone who can handle buyer enquiries, quotes and samples. It’s a good starting point if you have one country in mind and need to decide who to approach there.

What to have ready

Start with your product and target country. Before we begin research, we agree the specifications, price range, minimum order, capacity, lead times and any relevant certifications. These details determine which companies make the shortlist.

Know what you’re paying for before you pay

We confirm the product, target country and buyer criteria in writing before invoicing. The $600 USD fee is paid upfront. The seven-business-day delivery window starts once payment clears and we have the agreed product information.

Send us any duplicates, companies in the wrong country or entries that miss the agreed criteria within five business days of handoff. One round of corrections is included. See how we handle replacements and shortfalls.

When the shortlist is ready

Your team can put the shortlist to work immediately after handoff. The Brief identifies reasons to investigate, plus questions to confirm with the buyer. If you want help preparing and tracking outreach, you can commission a separate Buyer Outreach Sprint.
