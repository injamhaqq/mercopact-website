Frequently asked questions

Do I need an overseas sales office?

No. Buyer research and initial outreach can be conducted remotely, although local technical support or meetings may still be needed for some industries.

No. Research is paired with buyer-fit reasoning, outreach, qualification and a structured next-action pipeline.

Can you work with an existing prospect list?

Yes, when the data is appropriate to use. We can review relevance, gaps and previously contacted accounts before building a compliant campaign.

How is pricing decided?

International engagements receive a written scope and quote after we confirm the product, market, list size and level of support. Bangladesh packages remain separately available.
