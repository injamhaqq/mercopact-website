Discuss buyer outreach

Put your buyer shortlist to work

The $1,800 Buyer Outreach Sprint is for teams that already have a MERCOPACT Buyer Development Brief. We check the shortlist, write a message for each approach and follow up with the buyers you approve.

Need research and outreach together? See the $3,000 Buyer Development Pilot.

What’s included

We update the records for up to 12 priority companies on your shortlist. The sprint includes up to 20 approved initial approaches and up to 12 approved follow-ups, with up to two contacts per company where justified. You receive a weekly update and a final register with replies, quotation or sample requests, owners and next steps.

How approval works

You approve the sender, recipient, message and product claims before an approach goes out. Your team handles quotations, samples, pricing, technical commitments and orders.

Timeline and payment

The sprint runs for 21 calendar days from kickoff once the required inputs and access are ready. Pay $900 at kickoff and $900 at the agreed day-seven milestone, when you receive the updated buyer list, draft messages and approval record. We start sending once the second payment clears and you approve the messages. Delayed approvals or access can extend the schedule.

Take over the next conversations

At handoff, we review the replies, who owns each next action and which buyers are worth following up with.
