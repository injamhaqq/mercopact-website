Bangladesh market entry

Build commercial relationships in Bangladesh.

Mercopact supports international manufacturers and B2B companies entering Bangladesh through market intelligence, target-account research, buyer development, distributor search, decision-maker outreach and local commercial follow-up.

Local role

A market-development capability focused on commercial access—not a promise of guaranteed distribution or sales.

The challenge

A large market is not the same as an accessible market.

International companies often know Bangladesh is commercially relevant but lack a clear view of who buys, who distributes, who decides and how local commercial follow-up actually happens.

Mercopact structures the first commercial layer: market understanding, account mapping, outreach, meetings and disciplined follow-up.

What Mercopact is not

Clear scope creates better market-entry decisions.

  • Not a guarantee of distributor appointment
  • Not a guarantee of purchase orders
  • Not a substitute for competitive pricing or product-market fit
  • Not a replacement for the client’s technical, legal or regulatory responsibilities

Mercopact focuses on market intelligence, account access and development of qualified commercial opportunities.
