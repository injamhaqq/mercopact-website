Six essential workstreams

The disciplines required for reliable buyer development.

These are the competencies and review responsibilities a scoped service must cover.

01 / Commercial diagnosis

Understand the manufacturer's economics

Clarify the product, value of a new account, minimum order quantity, production capacity, technical constraints and authorised quote owner. Reject a market that the supplier cannot serve reliably.

02 / Market intelligence

Build a verifiable market hypothesis

Check original trade, company and product sources; timestamp research; identify competitors and practical access constraints; distinguish strong evidence from assumptions.

03 / Industrial account fit

Prioritise the right business and role

Explain why an organisation might use the product, which purchasing or technical function matters, what proof is missing and which accounts should be excluded.

04 / Responsible outreach

Write relevant, authorised communication

Check contact rights, jurisdiction, client approval, recipient restrictions and channel rules before proposing email or LinkedIn contact. Never present an uncontacted organisation as an interested buyer.

05 / Opportunity qualification

Know what a buyer actually said

Keep distinct records for researched account, relevant account, contact, actual conversation, RFQ and signed order. Record requirements, objections, decision owner and follow-up.

06 / Delivery assurance

Make work reviewable and correctable

Use a written scope, source-quality checks, documented acceptance criteria, a final quality review of every deliverable, issue correction, restricted client-data handling and actual delivery-cost measurement.

Professional execution

Quality controls before outreach, delivery and handoff.

GATE 01

Check you are ready to sell

Confirm product documentation, order economics, client authority and accessible quotation or technical reviewer.

GATE 02

Verify the research

Check every material account-fit claim against its source, record assumptions and review it by hand.

GATE 03

Approve client communications

Record contact authority, relevant law and restrictions, suppressed contacts, message approval and reply ownership.

GATE 04

Accept the work

Separate real response evidence from forecasts, resolve misclassified accounts and check actual delivery costs.
