Bangladesh Leather Goods: Who in the UK and EU Could Buy Them.

A research example for a Bangladesh-based maker of bags, wallets, belts and small leather goods looking for wholesalers and private-label brands in the UK and Europe.

Independent research example, not a client or buyer. The companies below were identified from their own public websites as research targets only. None has been contacted, none is a MERCOPACT client, and none has said it wants to buy from a new supplier. No outreach, RFQ or sales result is claimed.

1. The market hypothesis

Many UK and EU leather goods companies design their ranges at home and buy production from factories abroad. The five companies below were chosen because their own websites describe wholesale, trade-only or private-label leather goods. That does not show that any of them needs a new supplier.

2. Account-level fit signals

Public organisationWhat its website statesConditional fit for a Bangladesh manufacturerSource
M Golunski & Co (Golunski Leather Goods)

Brierley Hill, United Kingdom

Golunski says it is a family-run business trading since 1953 that sells trade only, designs in the UK and has products manufactured in the Far East by factories with exclusive UK relationships, audited and certified to ISO 9001, 18001 and OHSAS; ranges cover purses, wallets, card holders, cosmetic bags and glasses cases, and it offers a bespoke service for branding, colour changes or custom designs.Because it already outsources wallets and small leather goods to overseas factories, it could be a relevant prospect if a Bangladesh manufacturer can meet its audit standards and offer competitive small leather goods.Golunski
L.Credi-Peugler GmbH (L.Credi Munich)

Munich, Germany

L.Credi-Peugler says it has made handbags and small leather goods since 1953, supplies retailers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and offers private label projects covering design, sourcing and production in China, India or the EU, with optional handling of shipping and customs clearance.Its private label service already sources across several Asian countries, so it could be a relevant prospect if a Bangladesh leather goods manufacturer can match its quality expectations and price points for bags and wallets.Private label
Kinston (Asia Atlantic Groupe)

Vitrolles, France

Kinston says it has designed, manufactured and supplied leather goods and luggage since 1995 to mass retail, fashion and specialist retailers (2,000+ points of sale), manages sourcing, manufacturing and logistics from France, holds 1,200+ references in stock, and offers a white label service with a product development team. The pages opened do not name its production countries.Its white label and sourcing model could make it a relevant prospect if a Bangladesh manufacturer can supply bags and small leather goods at mass-retail price levels.Kinston
Dugros

Oud-Beijerland, Netherlands

Dugros describes itself as a wholesaler of bags, wallets, belts and leather accessories with 6,000+ products and brands such as Hide & Stitches and Charm London, offers private label production, and says its bags are made in its own factory in India plus certified and audited suppliers in Italy, France and China.As a wholesaler that already combines its own factory with outside suppliers, it could be a relevant prospect if a Bangladesh manufacturer can offer certified, audited capacity for leather bags, wallets or belts.Info bags
Saddler

Kungsbacka, Sweden

Saddler says it has designed and sold leather accessories (bags, belts, wallets) since 1986, sells to retailers through a B2B login, works within amfori BSCI since 2006, states that its Indian suppliers are SA8000 certified, ships from Asia by sea where possible, and links to the Leather Working Group.Because it sources leather belts and bags from Asian suppliers, it could be a relevant prospect if a Bangladesh manufacturer can meet its amfori BSCI and restricted-substance requirements, and ideally SA8000 and LWG standards.Manufacturing transportation · About us

Sources opened on 22 October 2026. None of the five names Bangladesh. The countries they do name are China, India, Italy and France. A Bangladesh maker needs a clear reason for them to add a source: price, quality, lead time or spreading risk.

3. Qualification questions

Product-specific fit

Which leathers, finishes, hardware and product types does each company sell, and can the factory match them?

Compliance and audits

Which standards do they expect (amfori BSCI, SA8000, Leather Working Group, REACH chemical limits), and which does the factory already hold?

Actual buyer authority

Who handles sourcing or product development at each company? No names are presumed here.

Reason to add a source

Price, lead time, minimum orders, quality or risk spread across countries. Only a real answer from the buyer turns an account into an opportunity.
