Which UAE packaging plants fit your equipment?

An expansion announcement gives you a reason to investigate. A production line’s width and materials tell you whether your equipment could work there.

Independent research · Sources reviewed 21 November 2026

These examples use public sources. Buying interest is unconfirmed. Each suggested next step shows what we would check before contacting the company.

What the companies publish

Hotpack Global

UAE · Packaging

Public evidence
On 20 May 2026 Hotpack announced a minority investment from GII to support expansion and international growth. Hotpack’s investment announcement
What to check next
Identify the operating facility, project stage and relevant production requirements. The investment is not proof of a machine order.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

Arabian Packaging

UAE · Corrugated production

Public evidence
The facility page describes 2.8-metre corrugators, multiple flute types and double-wall board production. Arabian Packaging’s facility details
What to check next
Match equipment to the installed line and verify width, material and technical support.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

ENPI Group

UAE · Diversified packaging

Public evidence
The group distinguishes flexible packaging and labels, preforms, films and bags among its business units. ENPI’s business units
What to check next
Find the business unit that runs the relevant material and ask who evaluates equipment for that plant.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

What we would prioritize

If you sell corrugated-board equipment, compare it with Arabian Packaging’s line first. For an expansion story, identify the facility, project and buying team before proposing a machine.
