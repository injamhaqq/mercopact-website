# MERCOPACT — International Commercial Intelligence, 12 October 2026

**Status:** Research branch, not live-site implementation. **Source standard:** 523 distinct names across public directories and official company sites; 328 structured directory snapshots; **23** specialist / platform records examined from official sites. A directory listing is not a verification of the business's present operations, current service scope, performance, or financial results. The 523 do not represent 523 direct competitors.

## 1. Executive Decision

**Chosen first-paid offer:** **MERCOPACT — Industrial Buyer Access & Channel Validation Sprint**

**Positioning:** "One product. One target market. The right commercial counterparties — researched, qualified, approached and tracked."

**What is sold:** A fixed-scope **28-day**, human-reviewed market-access engagement for established industrial and packaging-equipment manufacturers wanting distributor candidates and/or direct industrial customers in an agreed target territory. The deliverable is not a generic contact database: product-channel fit, the rationale for each target account, outreach execution, response qualification, and a visible next-action commercial pipeline.

**Initial international price to test:** **USD $1,500** for a 28-day pilot. This is an unvalidated MERCOPACT pricing hypothesis. **Expansion:** **USD $2,500–$3,500/month** for a managed export buyer-development desk, priced by market, client scope and resource demand. No guarantee of meetings, orders, distributor agreements or return on investment.

**First route-to-market recommendation:** Foreign packaging/food-processing machinery manufacturers seeking industrial buyers or capable commercial partners in **Bangladesh**, where MERCOPACT can at least investigate and qualify local fit more directly. This is an **international customer acquisition model** (foreign manufacturer pays); Bangladesh is the initial execution corridor, not a permanent geographic restriction. Expand to a second corridor only after client proof.

**Why this beat reactivation-only:** Reactivation requires a manufacturer to hand over its private CRM, existing leads, prior RFQs, sales history, and messaging permissions to a young company before trust exists. Also, cheap software already organizes follow-ups. Offer optional RFQ recovery when the client has the data and authorizes it, but do not make existing-data access a prerequisite to the initial sale.

## 5. Final client-facing offer (28 days)

**Ideal paying client:** Proven industrial equipment / packaging machinery OEM with production capacity, quote-ready specs, export documentation, and a director responsible for international sales. Has a real desire to enter or improve one market. Exclude firms lacking after-sales or technical support where required, products subject to unresolved regulatory restrictions, speculative sellers without ownership, and buyers demanding guaranteed POs.

**Scope and acceptance criteria:**
1. Product, pricing band, MOQ and delivery/service readiness briefing.
2. One selected target territory and agreed route: direct factory buyer, distributor, or manufacturer's representative.
3. **25** product-relevant target organizations, with visible relevance reasoning; **10** prioritized dossiers with product/channel fit, publicly documented evidence, potential conflicts and verified business contact routes where available.
4. Client-approved personalized business email and/or LinkedIn outreach to up to **25** priority organizations. Avoid unauthorized mass automation and circumvention of access controls.
5. Reply classification: no response, uninterested, information requested, qualified dialogue, relevant RFQ / sales opportunity. Do not label all replies qualified.
6. Client-facing opportunity register with dated follow-up ownership, requests for quotes/samples, objections and next decisions.
7. A strategy checkpoint after the first outreach sample and a closing review after 28 days.

**Quality commitment:** Replace accounts that fail the written ICP or whose stated facts cannot be supported with acceptable evidence. If the first authorized cohort generates no qualified response, revise the tested message or segment within scope. MERCOPACT commits to execution quality and honest evidence, **not commercial outcomes outside its control**.

**Commercial design to test:** USD 1,500; proposal and terms before initiation; optionally 50% deposit + 50% upon evidence-dossier acceptance. Make clear whether subscriptions, paid data, translation or travel are excluded. Ongoing desk is separately scoped USD 2,500–3,500/month, not an automatic upsell.

## 6. Homepage / cold-DM conversion concept

**Headline:** "Your next international buyer is not another name on a list."

**Subhead:** "MERCOPACT helps industrial manufacturers evaluate a new market, qualify the distributors and buyer accounts that matter, open relevant commercial conversations, and manage every credible next step."

**Mechanism:** One product → one market → commercial-fit map → client-approved outreach → replies/RFQs → tracked opportunity handoff.

**CTA:** "Review a sample market-access brief" (provide a **real and dated** sample with defensible sources, not imaginary existing customers).

**Risk reversal:** "Clear qualification criteria. An auditable shortlist. Defined 28-day scope. No fabricated buyer demand or guaranteed deals."

**Credibility rule:** Do not claim an overseas office, authorized manufacturer status, signed local distributors, prior €/$ million transaction volume, past client wins or partner meetings unless evidenced.

### Outbound opener — manufacturer-specific research, not generic pitch

Subject: `[Product category] — [target market] partner coverage`

Hi [Name], I noticed [verified, specific signal] while reviewing [Company]'s [specific product]. One market-access question stood out: [reasoned buyer/channel hypothesis]. I am preparing a short account-fit map for manufacturers selling this category into [country], including potential distributor conflicts and the procurement roles that matter. Would a one-page summary be useful? If not relevant, I won't follow up.

**Never invent** buyer demand, current deals, financial figures, existing market gaps or prior contacts. Stop if asked.

### Discovery script

Ask: "Which product line? Why this country? Who buys it? How many relevant dealers already cover it? What does the average order require technically? Who owns quotations and after-sales? What is the cost of pursuing the wrong partners? Can you approve named account research and legitimate outreach in your company's name?"

Sell **only if** the product and sales process are commercially ready. Send a two-page scoped proposal with acceptance criteria and decision timeline.

## 7. 14-, 30- and 90-day execution plan

**Days 1–3:** Check the actual MERCOPACT prospect file, ownership, privacy basis, manufacturer/product fit and accessible markets. Remove every lead not belonging to this ICP. Select 30 company accounts by observable export/distributor signals. Build three true one-page mini briefs.

**Days 4–7:** Send 15–20 genuinely customized first contacts. Ask permission to share research before pitching a retainer. Offer short product/country review calls. Track every message and opt-out.

**Days 8–14:** Reach 30–40 quality-fit accounts total, test two evidence-backed opening angles, conduct discovery with interested contacts, offer only a scoped paid 28-day pilot. Sales goals are hypotheses, not expected conversion rates.

**Days 15–30:** Seek first paid pilot; run it under client-approved operating instructions; collect honest response and buyer-fit evidence. Hold a review whether or not a deal closes. Calculate research hours, direct cost and collected revenue.

**Days 31–60:** Repair positioning based on objections; publish client-approved anonymized method/case proof if earned. Document qualification SOP and relevant partner-side issues. Expand to one additional buyer category only if pilot work supports it.

**Days 61–90:** Target one retained account plus a second paid pilot as planning goals, NOT forecasts. Evaluate churn, direct gross margin, cost per call and response qualification. Only then consider additional sector/corridor or employee.

**Decision gates:** No paid conversations → revisit recipient/trigger; positive replies but no meetings → improve opening offer; proposals but no wins → trust, scope and pricing; poor buyer fit → change segment; low delivery margin → narrow deliverables. Avoid premature outbound scale.
