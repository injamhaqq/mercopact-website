Independent research example

This example uses public information to show how we evaluate buyer fit. Current purchasing plans and supplier requirements should be confirmed before outreach.

What public disclosures can tell you

Company Public evidence What to verify Source
Stanley/Stella Its 2024 Brand Performance Check describes its supplier network and sourcing oversight. Knitwear category, target volumes, audits and current onboarding. 2024 Brand Performance Check
Lindex Publishes a factory list for its apparel sourcing network. Product fit for the relevant category, sourcing team and supplier requirements. Factory list
Kappahl Group Publishes information about suppliers and factories. Which categories the relevant team buys and what traceability evidence is required. Suppliers and factories
Engelbert Strauss Its 2023 social report describes workwear sourcing. Technical workwear requirements, volume and the route to supplier approval. Social Report 2023
Takko Fashion Provides a supplier contact route on its website. Whether the form covers your product and whether the relevant team is reviewing suppliers. Supplier contact form

Questions to resolve before outreach

Which product category and order size fit your factory? What audits and testing does the buyer require today? Who controls sourcing decisions? What specific reason would justify adding your product or capacity?
