Frequently asked questions

Short answers before you book a call.

What is in the free sample?

10 companies in your target market that could buy your product. For each: company, country, website and why it fits. We walk you through them live on a free 20-minute call. Decision-maker names, contacts and the approach plan are part of the pilot.

How long does a pilot take?

30 days. You see the first researched accounts within the first week.

What do you need from us?

Your product details, MOQ and capacity, certifications, the target market, and who approves outreach and handles quotes.

Who sends the outreach?

Outreach is personalised, and you approve every message before it goes out. We agree together whether it's sent from your domain or ours.

What if the pilot doesn't produce enough conversations?

Before we start, we agree in writing how many qualified buyer conversations the pilot should produce. If it falls short, we keep working at no extra cost for up to 30 more days. This applies when you approve messages within 2 working days, reply to buyer responses within 48 hours and send quotes within 72 hours. We guarantee the work, not the buyer's decision.

What counts as a qualified buyer conversation?

A qualified buyer conversation is a reply from a person at a company that fits the agreed target list who asks for prices, a quotation or MOQ, asks for samples or a catalogue, or agrees to a call or meeting. Not counted: auto-replies, "not interested" replies, job seekers, agencies and companies outside the agreed list.

How is pricing set and paid?

Pilots start from $1,500. The final price depends on your market, product and how much research is needed, and we confirm it in writing after your sample call. You pay 50% to start and 50% at day 15, once the first researched accounts and decision-maker map are delivered. Work starts when the first payment is received.

When do we get the full list and contacts?

The full account list and contact details are handed over to you after the final payment. If you start within 7 days of your sample call, we also add 10 extra researched accounts in a second market, at no extra cost.
