Frequently asked questions

Short answers before you book a call.

What is in the free sample?

10 companies in your target market that could buy your product. For each: name, country, website, why it fits, the likely buyer role and a source link. It takes about 5 days, costs nothing and you keep it.

How long does a pilot take?

30 days. You see the first researched accounts within the first week.

What do you need from us?

Your product details, MOQ and capacity, certifications, the target market, and who approves outreach and handles quotes.

Who sends the outreach?

Outreach is personalised, and you approve every message before it goes out. We agree together whether it's sent from your domain or ours.

What if there are no results?

You still receive the researched account list, the decision-maker map and a market report showing what worked and why. We don't guarantee orders, and we say so upfront.

How is pricing set?

Pilots start from $1,500. The final price depends on your market, product and how much research is needed, and we confirm it in writing after a short call.
