Commercial accountability

Measure what matters to your export team.

Buyer-account relevance

Count unique researched organisations meeting the agreed technical, product, geography and channel criteria. Each match requires supporting evidence.

Real commercial conversations

Separate attempted contacts, delivered messages, attributable replies and genuine buyer requirements. A reply is not automatically an opportunity.

RFQs and next decisions

Report only evidenced quote, sample or purchasing requests with a named client-side owner and next-action date. Purchase orders and collected revenue remain distinct.

Research time and effort

Track approved research effort, verification work and client review time. We don't claim time savings unless they are measured against an agreed baseline.

Commercial readiness

Record missing technical specifications, commercial constraints, distributor conflicts and support requirements before promising a buyer introduction.

Progress you can audit

Every deliverable should identify original sources, date checked, qualification status and what remains unknown.

Responsibilities under a written engagement

Know who is responsible for each commercial decision.

Market and account research

MERCOPACT checks the evidence, product fit and account selection, and documents any issues for your review.

Outreach and qualification

We write each message, check contact permissions and record every reply. Outreach is never handed to unsupervised mass messaging.

Client technical and sales team

The manufacturer approves specifications, quotations, meeting commitments, commercial terms and fulfilment decisions.

Before a project starts

The scope is written. The deliverables are checkable.

For a proposed international pilot, the contract should specify the product and market, research-account volume, qualification rules, permitted communication methods, weekly review cadence, quality-correction process, data protection, costs and handoff responsibilities. Any replacement commitment applies only within the agreed scope.

What we cannot promise: that a researched company wants to buy, a buyer will respond, a qualified RFQ will emerge, a distributor will sign or revenue will result. Those require independent buyer decisions and client-side execution.
