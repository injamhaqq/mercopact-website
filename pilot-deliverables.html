JuteBag

UK · Jute shopping bags

Public evidence
Tovu model JB005 is laminated jute, 35 × 45 cm with a 20 cm gusset and cotton-cord handles. Its published overseas print option starts at 500 units and eight weeks. JuteBag’s Tovu product listing
What to check next
Compare the bag with your dimensions, print options and total cost to deliver. JuteBag publishes these terms for its customers. Ask who reviews new suppliers and what terms they expect from a factory.

Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

Jutexpo

UK · Custom reusable bags

Public evidence
The customized-bag page gives 5,000-unit minimums for fully customized and UK-printed options, and describes printing in Kolkata. Jutexpo’s custom bag options
What to check next
Can your factory handle runs of this size and meet the standards Jutexpo publishes? Check whether its sourcing team considers additional suppliers before preparing samples.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

If your factory can make a bag like the Tovu, start by checking who reviews suppliers at JuteBag. Research Jutexpo if you can handle larger custom runs and explain what you would add to its existing supply.
