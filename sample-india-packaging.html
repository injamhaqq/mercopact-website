Which Indian packaging manufacturers fit your machine?

A carton finishing machine, a pouch line and a tube inspection system serve different manufacturers. These three examples show how to narrow the list.

Independent research · Sources reviewed 21 November 2026

These examples use public sources. Buying interest is unconfirmed. Each suggested next step shows what we would check before contacting the company.

What the companies publish

TCPL Packaging

India · Strategic partnership

Public evidence
On 17 November 2026 TCPL announced a joint venture agreement with Italy-based Brandart to explore Asia-Pacific packaging opportunities through a proposed Singapore company. Read TCPL’s announcement (PDF)
What to check next
Check which company would place orders, where production would happen and whether it has an equipment budget.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

Huhtamaki India

India · Flexible packaging and labels

Public evidence
Its India business describes barrier laminates, pouch formats and labeling processes. Huhtamaki India’s packaging range
What to check next
Find the plant that makes the relevant pouch or laminate. Check which materials it runs and who evaluates new equipment.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

EPL

Laminated tubes

Public evidence
EPL's July 2024 product page describes laminated tubes for personal care and other sectors, with printing and closure options. EPL’s laminated tube range
What to check next
Confirm the specific India plant and match the tube-making or inspection stage.
Status
Research only · buying interest unconfirmed

What we would prioritize

For a tube inspection system, check EPL’s plant and line first. For pouch equipment, start with Huhtamaki India. TCPL’s announcement gives you a reason to investigate its plans before proposing a machine.
