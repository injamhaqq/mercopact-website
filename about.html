Why Mercopact exists

Good companies often have a market-development gap.

They may have a capable product, production capacity and existing customers—but still lack a predictable process for identifying and developing enough of the right new buyers.

Mercopact is built around that gap: export-readiness analysis, market intelligence, account development, buying-committee research, Email and LinkedIn outreach, qualification, RFQ/sample/quote tracking and structured follow-up.
