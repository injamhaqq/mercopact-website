About

International buyer development for companies ready to grow beyond their current pipeline.

MERCOPACT is a Bangladesh-origin B2B buyer-development and market-expansion company. We help commercially credible manufacturers identify the right markets, develop qualified buyer accounts, reach relevant decision-makers and keep commercial opportunities moving.

Positioning

An external market-development and buyer-development capability for businesses that already have something valuable to sell.

Who's behind MERCOPACT

Injam Haq, Founder

I'm interested in how businesses find the right opportunities, and what makes them visible to the people who matter.

I started MERCOPACT to help manufacturers and specialist B2B suppliers reach the right international buyers. Every engagement starts with a specific product and target market. Why MERCOPACT exists

Good companies often have a market-development gap.

They may have a capable product, production capacity and existing customers, but still lack a predictable process for identifying and developing enough of the right new buyers.

MERCOPACT is built around that gap: export-readiness analysis, market intelligence, account development, buying-committee research, Email and LinkedIn outreach, qualification, RFQ/sample/quote tracking and structured follow-up.

Built around accountable commercial work

Five critical disciplines. One documented commercial process.

An international buyer-development program needs more than contact extraction. The engagement scope identifies who reviews each responsibility and which decisions remain with your technical and sales team.

01 · Market intelligence

Where to compete

Product-specific market assumptions, competitor context, realistic buyer categories and channel constraints.

02 · Account fit

Who deserves attention

Source-linked company research, relevance reasoning, verification gaps and clear exclusions.

03 · Commercial access

Who decides

Relevant company roles, contact research and permission-based, personalized business outreach.

Actual interest distinguished from contact activity, with product requirements and next actions recorded.
