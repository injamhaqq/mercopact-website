Keep your export buyer conversations moving

Managed Buyer Development is $3,000 USD per month after a completed Buyer Development Pilot or Buyer Outreach Sprint. We agree which companies to research and which conversations to continue for one product family in one market.

Monthly scope

  • Up to 20 newly researched companies.
  • Up to 25 approved outreach or follow-up actions.
  • Tracking for up to 30 active sales opportunities.
  • A weekly review of replies and next steps, and a decision together at month end about continuing.

What your team sees

For each open conversation, you see the last contact, who owns the follow-up and what happens next. Your team approves outreach and handles quotes, samples and commercial commitments.

Payment and renewal

Paid monthly in advance under a separate agreement. At month end we review which buyers still need attention and agree any work for the next month.
