Website and engagement terms

Effective 20 November 2026.

The website describes our services and research method. Paid work is governed by the written scope agreed with each client. Where the written agreement differs from this page, the agreement applies.

Fees and start dates

  • Buyer Development Brief: $600 USD upfront after scope confirmation. Delivery takes seven business days from cleared payment and complete agreed inputs.
  • Buyer Development Pilot: $3,000 USD for one product and target country. Pay $1,500 at kickoff after agreeing the scope and $1,500 when the shortlist, outreach plan and drafts are delivered for approval. The 20-account shortlist is due seven business days after cleared kickoff payment and complete agreed inputs. It includes 12 priority accounts for outreach and eight reserve accounts for handoff. The 21-calendar-day outreach phase starts after the second payment clears and agreed approvals and access are in place.
  • Buyer Outreach Sprint: $1,800 USD, with $900 at kickoff and $900 at the agreed day-seven milestone before live sending. The sprint runs for 21 calendar days once kickoff requirements are met. Delays in approvals or access may extend the schedule.
  • Managed Buyer Development: $3,000 USD per month, paid monthly in advance under a separate agreement after a completed Pilot or Sprint.
  • Specialist projects: Fees and schedules are agreed in writing.

Research and corrections

We record our sources and assess each account against the agreed criteria. If you identify a duplicate, wrong-market company or material mismatch, tell us within five business days of the research handoff. One consolidated factual correction round is included. We replace affected rows within five business days of confirming the discrepancy. If the agreed account count cannot be met, we discuss a written scope adjustment or a proportional refund for missing accepted rows.

Approvals and commercial responsibility

Clients approve outreach and remain responsible for product claims, quotations, samples, contracts and fulfilment. Deliverables are commercial research and agreed execution. Buyer replies, tenders, orders and revenue depend on the buyer’s decisions.

Handoff and continuation

We deliver the agreed work products and preserve the client’s ownership of its commercial relationships and confidential information. Additional work requires a separate agreement. Scope-specific handling, rights and obligations are documented in the written agreement.
