# MERCOPACT — Capability & Professional Skills Qualification Framework
**Dated 15 October 2026.** Internal qualification framework, not evidence of actual employees, approved certifications or paid client experience.

## Core characteristics
Commercial empathy, evidence-driven judgment, technical humility, clear writing, source verification, confidentiality, principled negotiation, reliability, cross-border awareness, financial prudence, responsiveness, accountability and continuous improvement.

## Competence grading — evaluate each person privately
- 0 = Not assessed.
- 1 = Explains principles, has not passed a practical test.
- 2 = Demonstrated independently on realistic simulated cases, checked by an independent reviewer.
- 3 = Proven in a client-authorized accepted project with retained source evidence, feedback and delivery logs.
- 4 = Repeatable leadership across multiple reviewed projects with documented improvement.
**Mandatory gate:** do not promise a client an active workstream until it has a confirmed authorized owner, reviewer, and score >=2 in each applicable critical competency. Scores 3–4 are forbidden without real client evidence. Never imply these roles are full-time employees.

## Operational drill sequence
**Drill 1 — Research:** one real publicly documented industrial product and ten potential account dossiers (demonstration only). Each record has source, date, actual product/process match, unknowns, exclusion, buyer role hypothesis. Independently check 100% before using as a sample.
**Drill 2 — Communication:** five mock emails and LinkedIn messages with permission basis and no actual sending. Grade relevance, verifiability, accuracy, tone, opt-out and compliance.
**Drill 3 — Qualification:** score twenty fictional responses; any false RFQ or verified-buyer claim fails the test automatically.
**Drill 4 — Handoff:** create a sample account report with buyer role, data source, response and dated next action. Confirm client approvals and no fabricated contact.
**Drill 5 — Contracting:** review draft SOW for client responsibilities, no order guarantees, acceptable research scope and jurisdiction limits.
**Drill 6 — Security:** test removal request, withheld-confidentiality scenario, bad email, access revoke and incident notification authority.
**Drill 7 — Economics:** time actual analyst effort, forecast delivery capacity and test whether a proposed fee can sustain the work.

## Work-owner assignment and skills records
Record assigned owner, assessor, role, evidence location (PRIVATE), exercise date, grade, expiry/retraining date, limitations and stop authority. Never commit employees' IDs, contact details, raw assessments, confidential CRM or credentials to public GitHub.

## Training order
Stage 1 — commercial discovery, manufacturing procurement basics and product fit.
Stage 2 — desk research, evidence integrity and buyer role mapping.
Stage 3 — English writing, objection handling and regulated professional outreach.
Stage 4 — private CRM workflows, SOWs, risk/security controls and reporting.
Stage 5 — actual supervised, appropriately contracted paid pilots.
Stage 6 — verified case studies, customer references and ongoing improvement.

## Public claims policy
Allowed: explain service workstreams, inspectable public research samples, documented criteria, SOW review and accountable approvals.
Not allowed without proof: decades of experience, a team size, fake client logos, ISO certification, legal representation rights, guaranteed deals, offices in multiple countries or claims that simulated drills are paid client success.
