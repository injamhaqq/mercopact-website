# MERCOPACT — Buyer Development Delivery, Evidence and Quality Controls

## Scope and authority
1. Verify signed client identity, real right to sell products, relevant trade registrations where required, jurisdiction and authorized contacts.
2. Obtain a written scope for **one product category and market**: product specification, minimum order/price constraints, capacity, delivery/warranty/support, intended customer categories, no-go accounts and applicable exclusivity.
3. Confirm that client owns technical claims, buyer quotations, pricing, closing, customs/import/export permissions and after-sales service. MERCOPACT is not automatically an authorized manufacturer's representative or exporter of record.
4. Obtain permission before communicating in the client's name or revealing commercially sensitive specifications.

## 5-stage buyer/account evidence taxonomy
- **L0 Researched company:** company name and source URL only; never imply commercial interest.
- **L1 Relevant target account:** evidenced product/process fit, geography and buying role; explicit fit rationale and freshness date. Still no buyer intent.
- **L2 Valid commercial contact:** business role and contact method independently checked, with legal source basis and suppression/opt-out flags.
- **L3 Qualified conversation:** attributable positive reply or meeting where role, product relevance, stated objective and next action are documented.
- **L4 Commercial opportunity:** authorized, attributable RFQ, sample, quotation request or purchasing project with known requirements and client-side next-action owner.
- **L5 Contract/PO:** client-confirmed signed documentation. **Payment collected** is a separate ledger event; never equate PO value with MERCOPACT fee.

## Evidence minimum per target
Unique account ID; company/legal name if available; country; product match; original official/association source URLs; published source date and research check date; buyer role hypothesis; fit confidence; exclusions/ambiguity; outreach-approved yes/no; message/response history in authorized private CRM (NEVER this public repo); client owner; last contact; next action and due date.

## Four quality gates
1. Source check: URL independently opens, sources support precisely the displayed claim, stale membership or unrelated affiliates flagged.
2. Product fit: model compatibility with substrate, throughput, specs, MOQ, pricing or market requires real technical validation.
3. Communication compliance: approved channel, verified recipient, specific relevance, source and suppression/opt-out, no LinkedIn scraping/automated DMs, region-appropriate email rules.
4. Commercial handoff: real reply, permission to respond, buyer requirements, next action, named owner, and CRM record.

## Service-level standards to propose (must be contracted, not silently claimed)
- All researched accounts must meet client-approved ICP criteria.
- Wrong-fit records are corrected or replaced within the priced quota.
- Every positive reply is labeled, attributed to a source, and reviewed before promised action.
- Weekly status notes distinguish outreach performed, reply, actual qualified dialogue, RFQ, proposal, contract and receipt.
- Unverified buyer demand, counterfeit PO, unverifiable mandate, undisclosed side commissions and unauthorized finance representations are prohibited.
- Publish buyer case studies only with named client review and explicit rights to disclose the relevant facts.

## Quality example
`Premiaflex Plastics` documented rotogravure manufacturing -> L1 *possible fit for compatible converting equipment only* -> NOT L3 or L4 because no approach or current demand is known.

## Change control
Scope changes and service pricing adjustments require written approval; never add public promises based only on speculative capacity calculations. Use current contract and actual email/CRM behavior as source of truth. Protect client data in approved private workspace only.
