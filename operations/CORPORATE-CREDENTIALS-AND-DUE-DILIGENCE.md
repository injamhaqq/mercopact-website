## Truth status
NOT_PROVIDED = missing evidence; SUBMITTED = received in an approved private workspace, not checked; VERIFIED = issuer or approved reviewer independently confirmed holder, scope and validity; EXPIRED = no longer current; NOT_APPLICABLE = approved reason. This repository contains no actual identity scans or sensitive business documents.

## Readiness levels
Level 0 — idea only. Level 1 — professional brand and publicly inspectable research-method demonstrations, operating templates available. Level 2 — verified legal identity, authorized signer, business mailbox, payment route, contract/legal/privacy owner. Level 3 — live assigned qualified deliverers, reviewer, client agreement, correct records and operational tests. Level 4 — real accepted paid engagement evidence and client permission for reference. Level 5 — separately independently certified, but only if a genuine certification exists.

Current public website / repository establishes some Level 1 material. Levels 2–5 may NOT be claimed from these documents.

## Private procurement packet (assemble after owner verification)
- Legal overview, registration and authorized signatory in correct jurisdiction.
- Working commercial email, official contact, real invoice identity, approved service address.
- One-page capability and service boundary statement, illustrative sample research with caveats.
- Project SOW, exclusions, confidentiality/terms, breach/privacy responsibilities.
- Technical reviewer/qualified contractor named where industry expertise is required.
- Client-approved cases and references only after written, revocable publication approval.
- Insurer/certification/partner evidence only when actually held.
- Review date and ongoing validity responsibility for every claim.

## Red-team tests
Would a buyer locate legal identity? Are names and invoice beneficiaries consistent? Does the operator have actual authority to represent the client? Is a sample merely research rather than market interest? Would a compliance officer approve contact sourcing and opt-outs? Is site contact deliverable and monitored? Can the team substantiate capability and offer written correction if evidence is wrong? Are subcontractors approved? Is there a named person who will stop unsafe work?

**Privacy:** Do not upload national ID, bank account data, unpublished references, contractor CVs or client records to public GitHub. Use an approved private vault and grant minimum access.
