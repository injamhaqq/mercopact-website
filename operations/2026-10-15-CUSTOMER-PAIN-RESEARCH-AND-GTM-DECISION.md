# MERCOPACT — Global Customer Pain Research & 360° Opportunity Decision
**As of 15 October 2026** | **Status: conditional strategic hypothesis, not proven customer demand**
**Owner:** commercial strategy | **Base:** latest production site and MERCOPACT pricing audit.
**Primary finding:** international buyer pipeline development is the strongest currently feasible entry opportunity. Re-engaging existing *authorized* warm enquiries may be a faster first paid use case when a client has genuine backlog.

## The actual question
Not "How many cold emails can we send?" but: **What costly, recurring sales or export pain can a company pay MERCOPACT to solve, without needing an international branch office, engineering team or unverified global distributor network?**

A buyer's experience should drive the offer:
1. We want customers in a specific overseas market.
2. We're unsure which accounts and roles fit our product.
3. Market data and generic lead agencies create busywork or weak-fit leads.
4. Our senior salespeople lose time researching and checking those accounts.
5. Prior referrals, show conversations, quotations and old leads are not owned or qualified consistently.
6. We want a qualified status and clear next step, not a thousand addresses and an activity dashboard.

**Evidence versus hypothesis:** (1)–(4) are supported directionally by export/sales surveys. (5) is a credible pain reported in trade-show research, not verified as prevalent among our paying ICP. (6) is the inferred job-to-be-done, requiring interviews.

## #1 recommended job-to-be-done
**International B2B Buyer Development Partner**: "For a specific product and international territory, show us which organizations genuinely fit, which business roles matter, which conversations are real, and what our team should do next—without wasting sales-engineer time on irrelevant outreach."

Three truths:
1. **Account fit** can be researched and evidenced, but is not **buyer intent**.
2. **Buyer interest** requires an actual attributable buyer response, and is not yet an RFQ.
3. **RFQ** is not a quote acceptance, signed PO or collected revenue.

### What to sell initially
**30-day scoped International Buyer Development Pilot** with one of two starting modes:
- **Existing opportunity recovery:** client supplies lawful and relevant inquiries, trade show conversations or old sales opportunities for qualification and manual follow-up. No marketing to people without a valid legal basis; do not inherit bad data.
- **New account development:** desk research in one approved foreign market; map a bounded set of accounts, document credible role match, get the client's authorization for copy and channel, handle actual responses and next steps.

These modes have different cost, customer proof and contact risks; quote them separately. Distributor/agent vetting is a later specialist option and may require a verified local subcontractor. No guaranteed meetings, buyers or POs.

**Potential pilot scope for *testing*, not a promised entitlement:** 20–30 source-checked accounts *or* 10–25 authorized existing enquiries; 8–12 detailed account evidence reviews; role map; research uncertainty; client-approved outreach; response classification; action owner and date; 30-day executive assessment. Volumes, fees and revisions must be agreed in writing.

## Ideal client + disqualifiers
**Positive:** proven sellable B2B product, existing export activity, healthy contract gross profit, data on specifications/MOQ/capacity/pricing, named executive and quote owner, chosen target territory, willingness to pay for a limited validation before asking for global scale.
**Negative:** not legally/export ready; commodity margins make the economics impossible; no quotation capacity, no technical documentation; demands guaranteed orders; expects MERCOPACT to sign contracts or certify a foreign distributor without verification; expects unmanaged email blasts; requires immediate import-export regulatory advice.
**Initial geographic test:** UK export-ready specialist manufacturers with a live growth trigger, compared against a small second-country cohort; this is a **hypothesis**, not a proven superior country.

### Triggers to investigate, not proof of intent
Recent exhibition attendance, public distributor recruitment, new export role hiring, cross-border product launch, growth-stage manufacturing capacity expansion, trade-association activity and explicitly requested new-territory coverage. Confirm the company's economic buyer and problem before pitching.

### What to ask in 15 founder interviews
- Walk me through the last overseas account you successfully opened.
- Where did the names and contacts originate?
- Which part of research, fit screening, intro and follow-up costs you the most time?
- Show one example of a good-looking lead that wasted time.
- How many event leads or RFQs remain unanswered or stalled?
- What has already been tried or purchased? At what true cost?
- What specific event would make solving this a priority this month?
- Who signs the supplier contract and controls the budget?
- What does a *qualified* buyer opportunity mean in your category?
- Would you pay for a source-linked research + follow-up pilot? What price and scope would you reject?
- What company or legal credentials must a new Bangladesh-based provider show?
- What would cause cancellation after the first month?

Treat respondents who don't buy as market evidence, not "bad leads."

## Operating and pricing red team
From MERCOPACT's own pre-existing model (hypothetical, NOT observed):
- $1,500 price, 104 delivery hours, $1,086 modeled direct economic cost => $414 or **27.6% modeled contribution before full CAC/overhead**.
- Actual profit could be substantially lower; don't assume $1,500 works.
- Time the first 10 full dossiers, 15 contacts, 20 message reviews and 5 follow-ups. Record real costs, then determine floor price and effective scope.
- US government $2,500 partner search is a reference that this task is bought somewhere, not a valid claim that MERCOPACT has equivalent capabilities, pricing power or legal eligibility.
- Payment and contracting entity identity, bank/wire/payment fees, duties/tax, refunds, jurisdiction and confidentiality still need validation.

## Decision gates (zero fabrication)
1. **Research gate**: Interview 10–15 prospective export/commercial principals and capture company/role/date (privately), detailed purchase trigger and existing solution.
2. **Commercial gate**: At least 3 meaningful fixed-price proposals and 1 signed/paid appropriately scoped pilot before claiming product–market fit.
3. **Unit economics gate**: Compare recorded work against fee and minimum required contribution *including* delivery, tools, owner time and CAC.
4. **Operations gate**: Confirm client permission to use their source data; outreach jurisdiction/legal basis; test CRM and opt-outs; confirm email DNS/receipt.
5. **Evidence gate**: Paid case studies only with customer permission; publish real work, not generic anonymous testimonial claims.
6. **Pivot gate**: If exporter buyers repeatedly indicate RFQ turnaround or distributor diligence is the funded urgent pain, reconsider the initial offer, but do not promise highly technical work without qualified delivery.

**Provisional #1:** international buyer-market qualification + commercial follow-through, beginning with warm account recovery where client already has suitable enquiries. Not mass email, not full-service global deal closing, not trade regulatory consulting.
