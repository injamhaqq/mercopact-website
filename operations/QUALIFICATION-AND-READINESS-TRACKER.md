## Assessment protocol
Statuses are NOT_PROVIDED / TEMPLATE_ONLY / SIMULATION_PASSED / OWNER_VERIFIED / EXTERNALLY_VERIFIED / CLIENT_ACCEPTED / REQUIRES_REPAIR. Every status must point to genuine dated evidence in the right private system. No retroactive declaration from a newly written template.

## What the initial operator needs to achieve
1. Complete practical source-check, account-fit, discovery, privacy, CRM, professional writing, technical escalation and SOW exercises.
2. Obtain independent review, fix major defects, document outcomes and identify actual human owners.
3. Test company mail, reply handling and privacy requests; do not switch FormSubmit live without receipt.
4. Verify legal business identity and cross-border payment ability before soliciting payment or signing.
5. Run a single bounded paid pilot, get client acceptance and time the work.
6. Obtain permission before publishing names, results, employee profiles, insurance, certifications or testimonials.

## QA cadence
Weekly: sample five accounts for supporting sources and relevance; inspect opt-outs, scope creep, next owner/date, billing status, false positive qualifications and security exceptions.
Monthly: management review of measured contribution margin, complaints, rework, client satisfaction, expired claims, legal/market developments and training gaps.
After every project: record scope vs. accepted outputs, actual labor, sources reviewed, positive replies versus RFQs versus transactions, and repeatability.

## Stop-work triggers
Unauthorized brand representation, false current buyer-intent claim, misclassified RFQ, unverified technical assertion, mishandled personal data, unapproved contact, fabricated credential, inaccessible real recipient mailbox, sanctions/commercial conflict or material project overrun without approved change order.
