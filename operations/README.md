# MERCOPACT — Commercial Operations & Evidence Registry
Date: 2026-10-12. **Template architecture only. This public repository must NOT contain real clients' confidential documents, raw prospect contact lists, negotiation details, personal data, account passwords, invoices, signed permissions, or private commercial terms.**

## What is ready
- `sample-market-access.html`: dated public demonstration of source-linked industrial account research, NOT an actual customer's success story.
- `operations/PRICING-CAPACITY-AND-MARGIN.md`: activity-based workload/cost stress test; every labor and supplier cost clearly assumed.
- `operations/SERVICE-DELIVERY-AND-QUALITY-SOP.md`: buyer-account qualification, client onboarding, quality gates, privacy, responsible outreach, evidence stages.
- `operations/VALIDATION-AND-METRICS.md`: measurement contract for acquisition, pricing and renewal, starting as Missing.
- `operations/CASE-STUDY-APPROVAL.md`: sign-off workflow and publication restrictions.
- `operations/RESEARCH-AND-TOOLS-DECISION.md`: evaluated research methods and agent/tool limitations.
- `operations/TEMPLATE-ACCOUNT-REGISTER.csv`: header-only CRM schema; keep REAL populated copies inside approved private CRM/workspace, never public GitHub.

## What is NOT ready or not proved
Real paid MERCOPACT client count, actual close rates, true renewal rates, realized costs, staff capacity, legal/registration details, paid annual revenue, client testimonials, existing mandates, cross-border compliance, automated contact form backend and effective privacy practices remain unverified. Do not substitute assumptions.

## Implementation gates
1. Seller approves one targeted industrial product/market and credentials.
2. Buyer-specific source verification and technical-fit QC on a dated record.
3. A commercially viable scoped proposal signed by the actual client.
4. Relevant contact basis and client-approved outbound.
5. Buyer replies/RFQs verified and recorded separately.
6. Client confidentiality and publication permissions managed privately.
7. Reconcile weekly hours/cash costs, not just messages sent.
8. Confirm actual endpoint receipt/CRM capture before paid website acquisition.

**Do not modify published package fees or 30-day pilot duration automatically.** Management must approve any scope and pricing changes after a timed paid delivery test.
