# MERCOPACT — Consent-First Customer Proof Framework

**There are currently no verified MERCOPACT client outcomes supplied for publication.** This file is a blank process template; it is not a testimonial.

## Evidence gate (all required before publication)
- [ ] Client contract and exact legal/client name verified privately.
- [ ] Deliverables accepted by named client representative.
- [ ] Quantitative metric defined before measuring (e.g., relevant accounts qualified, genuine RFQs, orders, repeat contracts; never equate with emails).
- [ ] Actual source evidence reviewed: CRM timestamps, client-approved call summary, attributable RFQ/contract documentation as applicable.
- [ ] Attribution discussed honestly: MERCOPACT activity versus client's existing team, seasonality or market changes.
- [ ] Client reviews every quote, logo use, dates, geography, commercial figures, relationship claims, screenshot and approved disclosure medium.
- [ ] Signed explicit permission includes duration, named channels, revocation/correction contact and anonymization agreement.
- [ ] Data is redacted for personal/contact/negotiation/private financial details.
- [ ] Source and signed approval retained in private compliant storage, **never committed to public GitHub**.
- [ ] Published case links to a named point of contact when permission includes this, or is clearly anonymized.

## Standard case-study structure (publish ONLY once approved)
1. Challenge in client's own confirmed words.
2. Product/market and agreed 30-day pilot scope.
3. Scope-delivery evidence: accounts researched, reasons for qualification, outreach and next-step outcomes.
4. Commercial results separately labeled: replies, qualified meetings, RFQs, signed POs, collected MERCOPACT fees — include measurement window and denominator.
5. Limitations and alternate explanations; no guaranteed or universal results.
6. Exact client-approved quote if consent exists.
7. Written client authorization date and safe display scope.

## Requests for consent (internal editable template)
"With your permission, we would like to publish a factual summary of the project and selected outcomes. We will show you the complete draft, remove any confidential or identifying data you do not approve, and will not publish your brand, quotations, buyer contact details or metrics until you explicitly approve the final version in writing. This permission is independent of the service contract."

## Not allowed
- Converting the website sample research about publicly listed third parties into a "successful client" story.
- Fabricated client logos or counters, stock photographs represented as customers, unverified ROIs, "we delivered $10m sales," or fictitious references.
- Assuming silence, a sales email response or preexisting relationship is consent.
