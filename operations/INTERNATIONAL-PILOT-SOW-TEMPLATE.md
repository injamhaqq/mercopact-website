## Human quality gates
1. Verify client's right to market products and actual authorized spokesperson/technical reviewer.
2. Check company sources, fit, date and gaps; reject unsupported positive intent or claimed purchasing stage.
3. Before contact: recipient-country rules, lawfully sourced business contact, opt-outs, sender-domain and inbox verification, approved message.
4. Record actual buyer response verbatim only in an approved private system; report summary with appropriate privacy.
5. Final report audit separates company researched, relevant account, actual positive reply, RFQ, quotation, signed purchase order and provider revenue.
6. Misleading claim, privacy incident, or unauthorized representation immediately stops the affected workstream and escalates.
