Company registration, licenses and authorized representation

MERCOPACT's website describes a Bangladesh-origin commercial development service. We do not currently publish an independently verified company registration number, license, foreign-office address, named manufacturer mandate or regulated-trade authorization on this website. Such credentials must be checked from original documentation for the legal entity and proposed activity before they are represented as verified.

If required for a commercial engagement, request the exact contracting entity, authorized signatory, invoicing details, applicable registration evidence, and any product- or market-specific agency authorization during pre-contract due diligence. A market-research service is not automatically a licensed import/export or customs service.

Customer case studies: publication only with evidence and consent

There are no verified past client results or approved testimonials presented in these examples. If MERCOPACT later publishes a customer case study, it should disclose the measurement period, work performed, attributable responses and commercial results separately, and show client logos, quotes or private commercial data only after written permission.
